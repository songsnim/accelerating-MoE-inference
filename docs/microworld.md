# Goal
Build dynamically and visually interactive microworld that faithfully represent the inherent mechanism of the given artifacts(concept, experiment, system, or idea), so that user can understand the whole idea, behavior and mechanism of the given artifacts.

# Rules
Low Floor, High Ceiling: Easy for beginners to start, yet complex enough to allow advanced exploration.

Embodiment of Powerful Ideas: Abstract concepts (e.g., geometry, physics) are embedded into the environment's rules and objects.

Immediate Feedback: Instant, non-judgmental visual response that turns errors into learning opportunities rather than failures.

Composability & Modularity: Simple primitives can be combined and abstracted to build complex structures.

Learner Agency: Driven by the user's personal goals and exploration rather than pre-programmed instruction.

Open-ended: The microworld does not have any intent to guide the user, it just represent the real world. It is just a side effect(actually the main, but hidden, goal) that user understand it faithfully.

Easy-to-understand: It should be so kind that even middle school students can understand. Each jargon and tech terminology should be explained with comment(popup when hovering).
